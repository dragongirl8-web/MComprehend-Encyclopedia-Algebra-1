Linear Equations

Welcome to the Linear Equations section of MComprehend! Linear equations are one of the most important topics in Algebra because they describe straight-line relationships between variables.

y = mx + b

In this equation:

m = Slope

The slope tells us how steep the line is and whether it rises or falls.

b = Y-Intercept

The y-intercept is where the line crosses the y-axis.

x & y

These variables represent points on a graph and help us visualize relationships.
